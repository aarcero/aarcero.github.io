Intel Energy Analysis

Analyzed regional electricity production, demand, and renewable energy availability to evaluate potential locations for a future data center.

Tableau • Data Visualization • Data Analysis

Project Overview

This project examined electricity production and demand across different U.S. regions to understand where energy infrastructure could best support the operation of a data center.

Key Findings

Five regions were identified as net energy producers: Mid-Atlantic, Northwest, Southwest, Central, and Southeast. The Northwest had the highest percentage of renewable energy generation at 52.25%, followed by Central at 41.01% and California at 37.45%. Of the top three regions for renewable energy, Northwest and Central were also net energy producers, making them particularly strong candidates for further evaluation.

Recommendation

I recommended the Central (CENT) region as a potential location for Intel's next data center. The region was a net energy producer while also ranking second in renewable energy generation at 41.01%. This combination provided a strong balance between energy availability and renewable energy use.

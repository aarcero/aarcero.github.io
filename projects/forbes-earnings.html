Forbes Earnings Analysis

Analyzed Forbes celebrity earnings data from 2005–2020 to compare the earnings of actors and actresses and examine how the difference in earnings changed over time.

Tableau • Data Visualization • Comparative Analysis

Project Overview

This project used Forbes earnings data from 2005 through 2020 to explore differences in compensation between actors and actresses. I used Tableau to compare earnings between the two groups and visualize how those differences changed throughout the period represented in the dataset.

Analysis

I compared the average earnings of actors and actresses to evaluate differences between the two groups. Looking at the averages provided a broader view of compensation than focusing only on individual celebrities.

I also examined the highest earners in the dataset. This made it possible to compare the celebrities at the top of each group and determine how individual high earners contributed to the overall earnings patterns.

Finally, I analyzed earnings across the 2005–2020 period to visualize how the difference between actors and actresses changed over time rather than treating the earnings gap as a single fixed value.

Visualization Approach

Tableau was used to turn the earnings data into visual comparisons. Rather than evaluating the dataset only through individual records, the visualizations made it possible to compare groups, identify top earners, and examine changes across years.

Takeaway

The project demonstrated how compensation data can be analyzed from several perspectives. Comparing averages provided an overall view of earnings differences, examining top earners showed the influence of individual celebrities, and analyzing the data over time provided context for how the earnings gap changed between 2005 and 2020.

Skills Applied

Tableau, data visualization, comparative analysis, trend analysis, aggregation, and communicating differences in earnings through visual data.

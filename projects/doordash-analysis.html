DoorDash Market Performance

Analyzed DoorDash delivery data across major U.S. markets to compare delivery performance, order activity, and customer demand and develop data-driven business recommendations.

Excel • PivotTables • Data Analysis • Data Visualization • Business Analysis

Interactive Workbook

Project Overview

This project analyzed DoorDash delivery data across Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, New York City, San Francisco, and Seattle. Using Excel, I compared order volume, delivery times, order prices, and cuisine preferences to identify differences in market performance and develop recommendations for improving operations and customer demand.

Market Analysis

I compared delivery performance and customer activity across six major U.S. markets. This made it possible to identify differences in delivery times, order volume, average order prices, and cuisine preferences between individual markets.

Business Focus

The analysis focused on translating market differences into potential business actions. Instead of treating every market the same, the findings were used to develop recommendations based on the specific performance and customer demand observed within each location.

Average Order Price

Cuisine Preferences

Customer Demand

American cuisine was the most popular overall, while Seattle had the highest percentage of sandwich-shop orders.

Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I recommended using driver incentives in San Francisco to help address its longer average delivery times. Increasing driver availability during periods of high demand could help reduce delivery times and improve customer experience.

I also recommended using consumer incentives in Seattle to encourage additional orders because it had the lowest order volume among the markets analyzed.

Promotions could focus on popular cuisine categories, including pizza and Mexican food, while market-specific preferences could be used to further tailor promotional strategies.

DoorDash Market Performance

Analyzed DoorDash delivery data across major U.S. markets to compare delivery performance, order activity, and customer demand and develop data-driven business recommendations.

Excel • PivotTables • Data Analysis • Data Visualization • Business Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzed DoorDash delivery data across Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, New York City, San Francisco, and Seattle. Using Excel, I compared order volume, delivery times, order prices, and cuisine preferences to identify differences in market performance and develop recommendations for improving operations and customer demand.

Key Findings

Across 50,036 orders, the average delivery time was approximately 49.9 minutes. Delivery performance varied across markets, with Boston and New York City averaging approximately 48 minutes while San Francisco had the longest average delivery time at approximately 55 minutes.

New York City had the highest order volume with 14,343 orders, followed by Chicago with 11,785 and San Francisco with 9,310. Seattle had the lowest order volume among the markets analyzed with 3,945 orders. Chicago also had the highest average order price.

Cuisine analysis showed differences in customer preferences across markets. American cuisine was the most popular overall, while Seattle had the highest percentage of sandwich-shop orders. These differences demonstrate how customer demand can vary between individual markets.

Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I recommended using driver incentives in San Francisco to help address its longer average delivery times. Increasing driver availability during periods of high demand could help reduce delivery times and improve customer experience.

I also recommended using consumer incentives in Seattle to encourage additional orders because it had the lowest order volume among the markets analyzed. Promotions could focus on popular cuisine categories, including pizza and Mexican food, while market-specific preferences could be used to further tailor promotional strategies.

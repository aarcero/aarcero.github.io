H&M Email A/B Test

Analyzed an H&M promotional email A/B test to determine whether changing the email subject line could improve customer open rates during a seasonal marketing campaign.

Excel • A/B Testing • Statistical Analysis

Project Overview

This project evaluated the performance of two promotional email subject lines for H&M. The goal was to determine whether changing the wording of the subject line could produce a meaningful improvement in customer engagement as measured by email open rate.

Experiment Design

The A/B test changed only the email subject line so that the effect of the new wording could be compared with the existing version.

Control Subject Line 20% off + free shipping
Test Subject Line Your last chance to score 20% off + free shipping.

Success Metric

Email open rate was used as the primary metric for evaluating the experiment. H&M's historical promotional email open rate was 18%, while the target open rate for the campaign was 20%.

Minimum Detectable Effect

Moving from an 18% historical open rate to a 20% target represents an absolute improvement of 2 percentage points. Relative to the original 18% rate, this corresponds to a minimum detectable effect of approximately 11.1%.

Analysis

I used Excel and statistical analysis to compare the performance of the control and test groups. The analysis focused on whether the observed difference in open rates was large enough to provide statistical evidence that the test subject line performed differently from the control.

Key Findings

18%

Historical Open Rate

H&M's historical promotional email open rate provided the baseline used to evaluate the campaign.

20%

Target Open Rate

The campaign targeted a two-percentage-point improvement over the historical open rate.

11.1%

Relative MDE

The difference between the historical and target open rates represented an approximately 11.1% relative minimum detectable effect.

+1.58 pp

Observed Lift

The test subject line produced an open-rate lift of approximately 1.58 percentage points compared with the control group.

Statistical Conclusion

The A/B test provided statistically significant evidence of an improvement in email open rate. Based on the experiment, the test subject line performed better than the control subject line on the campaign's primary engagement metric.

Recommendation

Based on the observed improvement and statistical results, the new subject line should continue to be used and monitored in future promotional email campaigns.

Future testing should also evaluate whether higher open rates translate into stronger downstream customer behavior, including click-through rates, conversions, revenue, and other measures of campaign performance.

Skills Applied

Excel, A/B testing, statistical analysis, hypothesis testing, minimum detectable effect calculations, experiment evaluation, and translating statistical results into business recommendations.

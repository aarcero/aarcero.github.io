Winter Sports Analysis

Analyzed Google Trends data for nine Olympic sports to identify cyclical patterns in public interest, event-driven spikes, and opportunities for seasonal content and marketing strategies.

Excel • Google Trends • Trend Analysis • Data Visualization

Project Overview

This project analyzed Google Trends search-interest data for nine Olympic sports. The goal was to understand how public interest in different sports changed over time and determine whether recognizable patterns appeared within the search data.

Search Interest Analysis

I compared search-interest trends across the sports to examine how their popularity changed over time. Looking at multiple sports together made it possible to identify differences in when and how strongly public interest increased.

Cyclical Patterns

The analysis identified cyclical patterns in public interest. Instead of remaining constant, interest in the sports changed over time and showed recurring periods of increased attention.

Event-Driven Spikes

I also identified spikes in search interest associated with major sporting events. These increases demonstrated how public attention can change significantly when a sport receives greater exposure.

Sport Comparisons

The visualizations allowed trends for different sports to be compared, including sports such as hockey and snowboarding. Comparing the series helped show that different sports can experience different patterns and levels of public interest.

Visualization Approach

I created charts that displayed search interest over time so that changes in popularity could be compared visually. This approach made recurring trends and sudden spikes easier to recognize than they would have been from reviewing the raw Google Trends values alone.

Business Application

The timing of search-interest increases can help inform when sports-related content or marketing campaigns may receive greater attention. The cyclical and event-driven patterns identified in the analysis provided insights that could be used to guide content strategy and seasonal marketing.

Project Takeaway

This project demonstrated that search-interest data can provide useful information about when public attention changes. By comparing nine Olympic sports over time, I was able to identify recurring patterns and event-driven spikes and connect those findings to potential marketing and content decisions.

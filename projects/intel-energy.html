Intel Energy Analysis

Analyzed regional electricity production, demand, and renewable energy availability to evaluate potential locations for a future data center.

Tableau • Data Visualization • Data Analysis • Business Analysis

Interactive Dashboard

Project Overview

This project examined electricity production and demand across different U.S. regions to understand where energy infrastructure could best support the operation of a data center.

Energy Analysis

I compared electricity production and demand to identify regions that generated more electricity than they consumed. I then evaluated renewable energy generation to determine which regions combined energy availability with a strong renewable energy presence.

Business Focus

The analysis was used to evaluate potential data center locations. Rather than considering energy production alone, the analysis compared both net energy production and renewable energy generation when developing the final recommendation.

Recommendation

I recommended the Central (CENT) region as a potential location for Intel's next data center. The region was a net energy producer while also ranking second in renewable energy generation at 41.01%.

This combination provided a strong balance between energy availability and renewable energy use.

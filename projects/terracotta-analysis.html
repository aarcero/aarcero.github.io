Terracotta Analysis

Analyzed customer survey data in Excel using XLOOKUP and PivotTables to identify top products, key purchase factors, and opportunities to increase online sales.

Excel • PivotTables • XLOOKUP • Data Analysis

Project Overview

This project focused on analyzing customer survey data for Terracotta. The goal was to organize customer responses and turn the survey data into information that could help explain customer preferences and support online sales strategies.

Data Preparation

I used Excel to organize the survey information and XLOOKUP to connect information needed for the analysis. This helped prepare the customer data for comparison and summarization.

PivotTable Analysis

PivotTables were used to summarize the survey responses and compare categories within the dataset. Instead of reviewing individual survey responses, the PivotTables made it possible to identify broader patterns across customers.

Customer Preferences

I analyzed the survey results to identify which products performed strongest among customers and determine the factors that influenced purchasing decisions.

Online Sales Opportunities

The results were then used to identify opportunities for improving online sales. Connecting customer preferences and purchase factors to potential business actions allowed the analysis to move beyond simply summarizing survey responses.

Excel Approach

XLOOKUP and PivotTables played different roles in the analysis. XLOOKUP helped connect information within the workbook, while PivotTables provided a way to summarize and compare the customer survey responses.

Together, these tools made it possible to transform the survey data into organized comparisons that could be used to identify customer preferences and purchasing patterns.

Business Application

The analysis connected customer survey responses to business questions. Identifying top products and important purchase factors provided information that could be used when developing strategies intended to improve Terracotta's online sales.

Project Takeaway

This project demonstrated how customer survey data can be transformed into business insights using Excel. Rather than only summarizing responses, the analysis focused on identifying patterns that could support decisions about products, customers, and online sales.

Skills Applied

Excel, XLOOKUP, PivotTables, customer survey analysis, categorical comparison, data organization, business analysis, and translating customer data into business opportunities.

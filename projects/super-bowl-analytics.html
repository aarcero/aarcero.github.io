Super Bowl Analytics

Analyzed historical Super Bowl television ratings and advertising costs to examine how audience performance and the price of advertising changed over time.

Tableau • Trend Analysis • Data Visualization

Project Overview

This project used Tableau to analyze historical Super Bowl television ratings and advertising costs. The goal was to visualize how both measures changed over time and explore the relationship between audience ratings and the cost of advertising during the event.

Television Rating Analysis

I visualized Super Bowl television ratings across the available years to examine changes in audience performance over time. Looking at the data as a historical trend made it possible to identify periods of growth, decline, and variation in ratings between different Super Bowls.

Advertising Cost Analysis

Advertising costs were also analyzed over time to show how the price of advertising during the Super Bowl changed throughout the dataset. This provided a second trend that could be compared with changes in television ratings.

Rating vs. Advertising Cost

I created a scatter plot comparing television ratings with advertising costs. This visualization allowed the two variables to be examined together rather than only as separate historical trends.

Visualization Approach

Tableau was used to create different visualizations based on the analytical question. Trend visualizations showed how ratings and advertising costs changed over time, while the scatter plot provided a direct comparison between the two quantitative variables.

Takeaway

The project demonstrated that audience ratings and advertising costs can be evaluated from both a historical and comparative perspective. Looking at each variable over time provides context for how the Super Bowl changed as a television event, while comparing ratings and advertising costs directly helps explore whether changes in audience performance corresponded with changes in advertising prices.

Skills Applied

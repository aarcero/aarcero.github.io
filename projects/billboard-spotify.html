Billboard & Spotify Analysis

Analyzed Billboard Hot 100 genre data and Spotify audio features to explore music trends and relationships between song characteristics.

Excel • Data Analysis • Data Visualization

Project Overview

This project explored music data from Billboard and Spotify using two different approaches. Billboard Hot 100 data was used to examine the proportion of songs associated with different genres, while Spotify audio-feature data was used to investigate relationships between measurable characteristics of songs.

Billboard Hot 100 Analysis

I analyzed genre information from Billboard Hot 100 songs and calculated genre proportions to compare how different types of music were represented in the dataset.

Converting the genre counts into proportions made it easier to compare categories relative to the overall dataset instead of relying only on raw totals.

Spotify Audio Feature Analysis

I also analyzed Spotify audio-feature data to explore relationships between characteristics assigned to songs. Scatter plots were used to compare quantitative variables and determine whether visible relationships or patterns existed between different audio features.

This part of the project focused on using visualization as an exploratory tool. Rather than looking at individual songs one at a time, the scatter plots allowed patterns across many songs to be evaluated simultaneously.

Visualization Approach

Different visualizations were selected depending on the type of question being answered. Genre proportions were useful for comparing categorical music data, while scatter plots were better suited for examining relationships between continuous Spotify audio features.

Takeaway

The project demonstrated how the same general topic can require different analytical approaches depending on the structure of the data. Billboard genre data provided a categorical view of music trends, while Spotify audio features allowed relationships between measurable song characteristics to be explored.
